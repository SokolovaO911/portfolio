Hi, I’m Olha, a junior UI/UX designer.

I work in Figma, Photoshop and Illustrator. I have one year of experience from my studies in UI/UX and graphic design.

What inspires me most are projects that help people and animals. I know this field not only from the outside: I have volunteered myself.

That showed me that the wish to help is not enough: the process of helping has to be simple, clear and accessible. People get involved more willingly when they understand what is asked of them, and this is where my work begins: an interface is the path from the wish to help to a real action, and I try to make that path short and clear.

Despite my love for social projects, I don’t limit myself to them. My third project, a redesign of the website of a company that runs adventure games, shows that the same approach works in any field.

I’m now looking for a full-time job in UI/UX design in Freiburg and the surrounding area: remote, on-site or hybrid.
